ARCHIVE SOUND AND NEW SCROLL SYSTEM

VISITOR SOUND
- Sound is OFF by default.
- Visitors can enable it with the Archive ambience button.
- Their choice is remembered in the same browser.
- Archives use low library ambience.
- The Widow's Wheel, maelstrom, pirate and slaver scrolls use sea ambience.
- Scroll links use a short drawer, parchment and unfurl transition.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

ADDING A NEW SCROLL
1. Open https://djobrienauthor.com/admin/ and log in.
2. Choose Archive Scrolls.
3. Click New Scroll.
4. Enter the next Display Order number.
5. Enter a lowercase URL slug, for example pirates-and-slavers.
6. Add the title, category and short description.
7. Upload the finished scroll under Scroll Image - Select or Replace.
8. The Full Article field is optional when the image contains the complete manuscript.
9. Save and publish. Netlify will rebuild the website automatically.

There is also an Author: add a new scroll shortcut on the Archives page. It opens the CMS login and new-scroll editor.

VERSION 2.8 - MANUSCRIPT OPENING AUDIO CACHE FIX

Root cause corrected:
- Version 2.7 renamed the WAV, but pages still loaded assets/sound.js under its old URL.
- Browsers and Cloudflare could therefore reuse the cached Version 2.6 JavaScript, which still selected the previous opening sound.
- The new 2.17-second WAV was also interrupted because navigation began after only 1.5 seconds.

Changes:
- Renamed assets/sound.js to assets/sound-v2.8.js and updated every HTML page.
- This forces browsers and Cloudflare to fetch the new event logic.
- Increased manuscript navigation delay to 2.3 seconds, allowing the complete 2.17-second sound to finish.
- Removed both obsolete parchment-opening WAV files from the complete website package.
- Kept the selected file: assets/audio/parchment-opening-zapsplat-v2.wav.
- Quill remains independent of pointer, wheel, touch and scroll events.

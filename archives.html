Welcome, traveller

Five centuries have passed since the events of the Dark Lair Trilogy. Brihanon, Arch Scribe of the Western Isles, preserves what remains of that age from the King’s Tower at Kan-Ta.

The lands of his day are peaceful. The western kingdoms commune agreeably, and no dragon or wyvern has been seen in a hundred years. Yet the old accounts remain, incomplete and sometimes contradictory.

D.J. O'Brien Author Website - Version 1.4

Objective
Add the first real ambient audio layer to the home page.

Files changed
- assets/sound.js

Changes
- Added generated mountain-wind ambience on the home page.
- Added a very quiet magical crystal hum beneath the wind.
- Sound begins after the visitor's first browser interaction, subject to browser autoplay rules.
- Existing crystal control, mute toggle, volume slider and saved preferences remain in place.
- Turning sound off fades and stops the ambience.
- Turning sound back on restarts the home ambience.
- No external or copyrighted audio files are used.

Not included in this version
- Archive ambience
- Scroll and quill effects
- Battle sounds
- Ravens, thunder or bells

AMBIENT SOUND UPDATE

Implemented:
- Sound enabled by default at 24% volume.
- Glowing crystal sound control at the lower-right of every page.
- On/off button and adjustable 0-100% volume slider.
- Volume and mute choice remembered in the visitor's browser.
- Page-specific wind, forest, fire and crystal ambience.
- Parchment and quill sounds for future scroll/manuscript elements using:
  .scroll, .archive-scroll, .manuscript, [data-scroll], or [data-manuscript]
- Brief battle ambience when war-related sections enter view.
- Effects begin within a few seconds and repeat at short, varied intervals.
- All audio in assets/audio was created specifically for this build.

BROWSER NOTE
Modern browsers often block audible autoplay until a visitor clicks, taps or presses a key.
The site attempts autoplay, then begins immediately after the first interaction if blocked.
